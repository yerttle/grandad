      Grandad's Cold Snap

      The boiler's packed in on the coldest night since 1963, and Grandad won't budge from his chair. Race round the house and fetch his cosy things before his temperature drops to 35°C and he gets hypothermia. Watch out: the colder he gets, the grumpier he gets, and he's got a rolled-up newspaper.

      Players
      How cold is it?

      How to play

      1. Roll the dice, then choose which way to go round the house: clockwise or anticlockwise.
      2. Land exactly on one of Grandad's things to pick it up. There's one hidden in every room.
      3. Pass or stop on a yellow Pop in to Grandad door to hand over everything you're carrying.
      4. When Grandad swings his paper, press Space as the marker crosses the green to duck. Get hit and you'll drop something, or miss a go.
      5. Deliver all 8 cosy things before he cools to 35.0°C. Custard creams let you re-roll.
      Keys: Space rolls, ← → pick a direction, R re-rolls, M mutes.
