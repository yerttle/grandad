      Grandad's Cold Snap

      The boiler's packed in on the coldest night since 1963, a travelling fair has somehow set up in Grandad's house, and all his cosy things are on the stalls as prizes. Race round the rooms, win them back at the fairground games, and get them to Grandad before his temperature drops to 35°C and he gets hypothermia. Watch out: the colder he gets, the grumpier he gets, and he's got a rolled-up newspaper.

      How to play

      1. Roll the dice, then choose which way to go round the house: clockwise or anticlockwise.
      2. Land in a room that still has its prize and you play that room's fairground game. Win it to take Grandad's thing.
      3. Pass or stop on a yellow Pop in to Grandad door to hand over everything you're carrying.
      4. When Grandad swings his paper, press Spacetap Duck! as the marker crosses the green to duck. Get hit and you'll drop something, or miss a go.
      5. Once a room's prize has been won, a parcel floats over its stall: land anywhere in that room and you have a rummage. You might find custard creams, favourite points, a lucky charm... or a sock.
      6. Deliver all 8 cosy things before he cools to 35.0°C. Custard creams let you re-roll.
      7. Grandad keeps score of who's his favourite: points for winning stalls, delivering things and ducking his paper. Playing together? Land on another player's square and you can pinch what they're carrying, if Grandad isn't looking.

      Just want the fairground?

      Have a practice go on any of the 13 stalls. Nothing counts, and it uses the difficulty you've picked.
